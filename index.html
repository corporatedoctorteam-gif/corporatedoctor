Questions, answered

Before you take the next step.

A few things healthcare professionals commonly want to know before exploring a non-clinical career.

Is Corporate Doctor only for MBBS doctors?

No. The platform is designed for MBBS, BDS, AYUSH, BPT, Nursing, Pharmacy, Biotechnology and Life Sciences professionals exploring careers One Degree. Infinite Possibilities.

Can I browse jobs for free?

Yes. You can browse the free job feed and use the search and filters. A free account can unlock the full vacancy list and lets you save jobs.

Are all jobs verified?

No. We distinguish between curated and verified listings. Curated means selected for relevance; the verified badge is reserved for vacancies explicitly checked by Corporate Doctor.

Do I need clinical experience to move into a non-clinical role?

It depends on the role and employer. Each vacancy should be evaluated against its stated eligibility and experience requirements.

Will a Corporate Doctor course guarantee me a job?

No course can honestly guarantee a job. The goal is to help you understand the role, build relevant skills and prepare more effectively for opportunities.

I'm completely confused. Can someone guide me?

Yes. You can book a 1:1 Career Clarity Call if you want personalised guidance on your options and next steps.
