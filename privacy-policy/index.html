Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Corporate Doctor collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you use corporatedoctor.in, our vacancy and career-discovery services, and related career programs or guidance services.

Important: This is a practical website privacy notice, not a substitute for legal advice. We recommend having the final policy reviewed by a qualified lawyer for your business structure and applicable Indian data-protection requirements.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, account information, career preferences, qualification or experience information that you choose to provide, job interactions, course or guidance enquiries, and information you submit through forms.

2. How we use information

  • To create and maintain your account and authenticate you using email OTP.
  • To provide career discovery, vacancy browsing, saved-account functionality and related services.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide 1:1 career guidance or course-related support.
  • To communicate important account, service, payment or support information.
  • To understand website usage, improve the platform and measure marketing and conversion performance.
  • To prevent misuse, fraud, security incidents or unauthorised access.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Analytics and advertising technologies

Corporate Doctor uses analytics and advertising measurement tools, including Google Analytics 4 and the Meta Pixel. These tools may receive information about website interactions, device/browser information and conversion events in accordance with their own policies. We use this information to understand traffic, improve the website and measure advertising performance.

4. Third-party services

Some services are delivered through third parties. For example, course and 1:1 guidance purchases are handled through our Graphy/academy environment, and payments may be processed by payment providers. Job application links may take you to an employer's website. Those third parties have their own terms and privacy practices.

5. How we protect information

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the services we provide. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including account administration, service delivery, records, dispute handling, security and legal obligations.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information where applicable, or to ask questions about how your information is handled. We will process requests subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements.

8. Children

Our services are intended for adults and healthcare learners/professionals. We do not knowingly design the platform for children.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@corporatedoctor.in.
